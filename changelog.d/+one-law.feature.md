The built-in table is gone: the phronesis pack is the one law, on the line and on every command the walk finds inside it (wrappers, `sh -c` scripts, substitutions, `find -exec` payloads). The pack names its own severity: an irreversible hit denies, and a hit it tags as a warning, or any other hit, runs with a `warn` reason. The host hands the pack facts, not rules: paths inside the work tree as relative ones, a link out of `/tmp` by its target, a fresh temp or configure scratch as a temp, `$LJOS_POLICYD_CONFIG` as its path, and whether a push rewrites the checkout's default branch. Verdict tokens are unchanged.
