A long argument is judged on what it says. A word of 512 bytes or more was refused before the pack read it: each word was copied into a 512-byte path buffer, and a Cap'n body stopped at 64 KiB, so `echo` of a long program was denied with no reason of its own. The word is kept and handed to the pack. Past the size of an argument vector the refusal is `field-too-long` and a reason, not a bare `phronesis`.
