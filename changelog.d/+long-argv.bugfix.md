A long argument is judged on what it says. A word of 512 bytes or more was refused before the pack read it, so `echo` of a long program was denied with no reason of its own. Release and test builds link phronesis v0.2.3, which keeps each word up to 128 KiB and up to 32768 words. Past those limits the line is `field-too-long` and the host's reason, as in `field-too-long: argv word exceeds 131072 bytes`.
